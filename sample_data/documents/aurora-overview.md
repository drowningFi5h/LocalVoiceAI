# Aurora field station

Aurora is a fictional coastal research station used only as a demonstration dataset. It opened in 2022 and houses six researchers. The station studies tidal water quality and coastal weather. The site coordinator is Mira Chen.

## Power and water

Aurora uses a 12-kilowatt solar array and a 40-kilowatt-hour battery. A diesel generator is reserved for emergencies. The battery warning threshold is 25 percent. Below that threshold, staff pause nonessential laboratory equipment and notify the coordinator.

The rainwater tank holds 8,000 liters. Drinking water passes through a sediment filter and ultraviolet treatment. The sediment filter is replaced every 30 days. The station keeps a seven-day reserve of bottled drinking water.

## Daily operations

The morning briefing starts at 08:30 local time. Field teams work in pairs and record departure and return in the field log. Quiet hours run from 22:00 to 06:00. Visitors require advance approval from the coordinator and a safety briefing on arrival.
