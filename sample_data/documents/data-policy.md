# Research data policy

## Storage and retention

Raw sensor observations are retained for five years. Derived analysis reports are retained for three years. Store raw readings in the station archive and back them up nightly at 02:00 local time to an encrypted external drive. Once per week, verify restoration of one randomly selected backup file.

## Sharing and privacy

Public datasets must remove staff names, personal contact details, and precise nesting-site coordinates. The coordinator approves every public release. Publish approved datasets under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license. Never publish incident logs or access credentials.

## Quality control

Mark suspicious readings with a quality flag rather than deleting them. A reviewer records the reason and any correction in a separate annotation. Reports must state the sensor calibration version and cite the raw dataset identifier. The data steward, Arun Patel, checks completeness every Friday.
