# Safety and communications

## Emergency response

The assembly point is the orange marker beside the north gate. The first-aid cabinet is in the kitchen. For a medical emergency, contact the coordinator and use the satellite phone to call the regional rescue desk. The fictional rescue desk call sign is Harbor Rescue; this dataset contains no real emergency telephone numbers.

Outdoor work stops when sustained wind exceeds 45 kilometers per hour or lightning is observed within 10 kilometers. Resume fieldwork only after coordinator approval. During a power warning below 25 percent battery, suspend nonessential laboratory loads and notify Mira Chen.

## Communications outage

The station's primary connection is a microwave radio link. If it fails, switch to the satellite terminal and log the outage time. If both links fail, suspend remote field trips and check in over VHF channel 12 every 30 minutes. Never leave a field team without a working radio.

## Incident reporting

Record an incident in the safety log within two hours. Include time, location, people involved, immediate actions, and follow-up owner. The coordinator reviews open incidents at the next morning briefing.
