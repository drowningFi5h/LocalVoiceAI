# Sensor handbook

## TideSense T4

The TideSense T4 measures water salinity and temperature. Salinity accuracy is plus or minus 0.2 practical salinity units. The T4 is calibrated every 14 days using a certified 35 PSU reference solution. Rinse the probe with fresh water after each field session. Replace the probe if it fails two consecutive calibration checks.

## SkyWatch S2

The SkyWatch S2 measures wind speed and air temperature. The S2 is calibrated every 30 days. During calibration, compare its temperature reading with the reference thermometer for 10 minutes. Mount it two meters above ground and at least five meters from buildings.

## Sampling and records

Both sensors record observations every 60 seconds. Upload readings to the station archive at 18:00 local time each day. Each observation includes the sensor ID, UTC timestamp, measured value, and calibration version. Keep raw observations unchanged; corrections are stored as separate annotated records.
